Import hours

Bring in hours you tracked somewhere else. In Excel, save the sheet as a CSV file, then choose it here. The app reads these columns: Date, Type, Subcategory, Minutes or Hours, Format, Client code, Note and Supervisor. Only Date, Type and Minutes or Hours are required.

The template has two sample rows that show the format. Replace them with your hours.

Types that are not Direct client hours, Indirect hours, Supervision or one of your own types are added as new types. Subcategories not already in the app are added to the matching type. Nothing already in the app is changed.
